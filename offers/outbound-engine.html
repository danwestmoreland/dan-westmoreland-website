Why does it take 5 weeks to start sending?

New inboxes need 3-4 weeks of warmup before real volume. Skipping warmup is the most common reason cold email lands in spam. The build itself is done in 2 weeks.

Which tools do you use?

Google Workspace inboxes, Smartlead or Instantly for sending and warmup, your existing CRM, and Clay or Apollo for the list. You pay the tools directly, so nothing is marked up.
