A fit if

  • You're funded, $1M-$15M ARR, and there's no CMO yet
  • Sales is outpacing inbound and pipeline depends on outbound
  • You have a hungry junior team that needs senior direction
  • You want AI leverage built in, not bolted on later

Not a fit if

  • You want a strategy deck and no one to execute it
  • Product-market fit is still an open question

Frequently asked

Before you book.

How much time do you spend with us?

Typically 1-3 days a week, depending on scope. Enough to own the plan and ship, not so much that you're paying for a full-time seat.

Will you manage our marketing team?

Yes, and coach them. The goal is a team that runs the playbook without me by the end of the engagement.

What does it cost?

Three tiers: Advisor at $3,000 a month, Operator at $5,500, and Embedded from $7,500. Full details are on the pricing page.

When should we hire full time instead?

When the playbook is proven and the job is scaling it. I'll help you write the role, interview, and hand off.

Founders call me when something's stuck. What's stuck?

Thirty minutes. Bring your numbers. If it's not a fit, I'll tell you and recommend someone who is.
