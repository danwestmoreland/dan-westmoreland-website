Frequently asked

Before you book.

Do we need to buy new tools?

Usually not many. Most teams already pay for a CRM, an enrichment source, and an LLM. The gap is the workflow between them. I build on what you have first and only add tools that pay for themselves.

Is this an AI SDR that replaces our reps?

No. It's the research, signal detection, and first-draft work your reps hate doing, automated. Your reps spend their time on conversations instead of list building.

How fast do we see results?

The first workflow is usually live in two to three weeks. Outbound that needs new sending domains takes three to four weeks longer because the inboxes have to warm up first.

What tools do you work in?

Clay, HubSpot, Salesforce, Instantly, Smartlead, n8n, Zapier, and the major LLM APIs. If it has an API, I can usually wire it in.
