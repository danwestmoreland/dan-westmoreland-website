Frequently asked

Working with me in Atlanta.

Do you meet clients in person in Atlanta?

Yes. I'm based in Atlanta, in the Old Fourth Ward, and I meet local clients in person for kickoffs, team sessions, and the occasional working lunch. Most of the week-to-week work is remote either way.

What does a fractional CMO in Atlanta cost?

My retainers are $3,000 a month for Advisor, $5,500 for Operator, and $7,500 to $10,000 for Embedded, with a three-month minimum. Fixed-price builds start at $2,500. Prices are the same whether you're in Atlanta or anywhere else in the US.

What kinds of Atlanta companies do you work with?

B2B software companies with 20 to 200 people and no senior marketing leader in seat: funded SaaS, bootstrapped vertical software, and PE-backed companies that need pipeline, not a rebrand.

What is GTM engineering, and why does an Atlanta company need it?

GTM engineering is building the systems that create pipeline: enrichment, intent signals, outbound, and AI research and drafting wired into your CRM. Georgia companies hire for the role at a median of about $130,000. Most need one person's worth of it. That's the fractional case.

Are you a fractional CMO or a GTM engineer?

I've never carried the CMO title, and I won't pretend otherwise. I've run demand generation as a Director, VP, and Head of at five software companies, which is the job a fractional CMO is hired to do. Most fractional CMOs in Atlanta bring strategy and a deck. Most GTM engineers bring builds and no judgment about which build matters. I bring the plan and I build the engine that executes it.
