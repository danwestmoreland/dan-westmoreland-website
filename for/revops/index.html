Frequently asked

Before you book.

Do we have to switch tools?

Usually not. Most teams already pay for a CRM, an enrichment source, and a sequencer. The gap is the plumbing between them. I add tools only when they pay for themselves.

How do you handle deliverability?

Separate sending domains, two to three weeks of warmup, tracking off on new domains, plain text, and volume caps per inbox. The main domain never sends cold email.

Can you work inside our Clay workspace?

Yes, and I prefer it. You keep the tables, the credits, and the history. I document what I change.

What does ongoing support look like?

Every build includes a 30-day tune-up window. After that, the Advisor retainer ($3,000 a month) covers a weekly call and async access if you want someone on call.
