Frequently asked

Before you book.

Why not just hire a full-time head of marketing?

You probably should, eventually. A good one takes three to six months to find and another three to ramp. The retainer bridges that gap and leaves them a working engine instead of a cold start.

Which tier do founders usually start with?

Operator, at $5,500 a month. Advisor ($3,000) works if you already have someone who can execute. Embedded ($7,500 to $10,000) is for when you want me owning the pipeline number outright.

Do you work with pre-revenue companies?

No. Below about $2M ARR or a Series A, the retainer is too large a share of your budget to be a good decision, and I'd rather tell you that than take the money.

What if we need something smaller first?

Fixed-price builds: an Outbound Engine Install is $3,500, a Clay Credit Audit is $2,500, an AI SDR Install is $12,500. Each one is a complete system handed over, no retainer required.
