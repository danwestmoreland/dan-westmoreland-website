What a GTM engineer actually does

Marketing ops keeps the stack running. RevOps reports on it. A GTM engineer builds new revenue systems on top of it: a visitor identification pipeline that routes a pricing-page hit to the right rep inside an hour, an outbound engine on warmed domains that books meetings while the team sleeps, an AI SDR that researches an account, drafts the first touch, and triages the reply.

It's a new title with old roots. At Curve Dental I built the visitor and intent signals into an AI SDR that added 6 to 10 demos a month with no new headcount, and took inbound demos from 60 a month to 200+. At Deputy I built the content engine that took a new market from 0 to 100K monthly blog visitors and 800 trial requests a month. The title didn't exist yet. The work did.

Frequently asked

About GTM engineering.

What is a GTM engineer?

A GTM engineer builds the systems that create pipeline: data enrichment, intent signals, outbound sequencing, AI research and drafting, and the CRM plumbing that ties them together. Marketing ops keeps the stack running. RevOps reports on it. A GTM engineer builds new revenue systems on top of it.

What does a fractional GTM engineer cost?

Fixed-price builds start at $2,500 for a Clay Credit Audit, $3,500 for an Outbound Engine Install, and $12,500 for an AI SDR Install. Ongoing retainers run $3,000 to $10,000 a month. Every price is on the pricing page.

Why hire fractional instead of full time?

Most companies that have a GTM engineer have exactly one, and the role's median salary is around $150,000. A fractional engineer gives you the builds without the seat, and hands over a documented system your team can run.

What tools do you build with?

Clay, HubSpot, Salesforce, Smartlead, Apollo, n8n, Zapier, visitor identification tools like RB2B, and Claude for research and drafting. Tools stay in your name and on your card. Nothing is marked up.

How long does a build take?

A Clay audit is five days. An outbound engine is about two weeks plus inbox warmup. An AI SDR install is four to six weeks, most of it spent on the signals and the handoff into your CRM.

Do you work with companies outside Atlanta?

Yes. Nearly all of the work is remote. I'm based in Atlanta and meet local clients in person when it helps.
