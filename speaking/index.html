Talks

The GTM engineer: what the title means and what to build first

Keynote or breakout, 30 to 45 min

The job description is new; the work isn't. What a GTM engineer actually builds, how it differs from marketing ops and RevOps, the four systems to build in order, and why most companies need one person's worth of it rather than a seat. Live walkthrough of a visitor-ID-to-Slack pipeline built on free tools.

AI in B2B marketing without replacing the team

Keynote or workshop, 30 to 90 min

At Curve Dental I wired intent signals into an AI SDR that added 6 to 10 demos a month with no new headcount, and inbound demos went from 60 a month to 200+. What worked, what embarrassed us, where humans stayed in the loop, and the honest cost. Attendees leave with a build plan for one workflow.

Brand equals demand: the blog post that outworked an SDR team

Keynote, 20 to 30 min

At Marketo, one how-to article generated dozens of inbound calls a week from people who had bought the software and didn't know what to do with it. At Deputy, the same idea took a new market from zero to 100K monthly blog visitors and 800 trial requests a month. The thesis, the mechanics, and how it changes with AI search.

Fractional, full-time, or AI-augmented: the honest math of the marketing seat

Panel or founder session, 30 min

For founders at $2M to $20M ARR deciding who should own marketing. What each option costs, what each one can't do, and the questions to ask before hiring any of them. I've been on three sides of this and I show the prices.

Good at it: succeeding as a neurodivergent person in a 9-to-5

Keynote or fireside, 30 to 45 min

I'm ADHD and I'm good at it. Ten years of senior marketing roles, built on systems that work with how my brain runs instead of against it: what I automate, what I refuse, how I structure a week, and what I tell a new manager in week one. For ERGs, all-hands, and anyone who has been told to "just focus."

Managing a neurodivergent teammate without wasting either of you

Manager session or workshop, 45 to 90 min

For managers and leadership teams. The small changes that turn a frustrating report into your best one: how to brief, how to check in, what to measure, and which "best practices" quietly punish neurodivergent people. Told from both chairs, since I've been the report and the manager.

Frequently asked

Before you book.

What do you speak about?

GTM engineering and what it actually looks like inside a company; how to implement AI in B2B marketing and sales without replacing the team; building inbound engines from zero; the honest math of fractional versus full-time marketing leadership; and two talks on neurodivergence at work, one for the person and one for their manager. Every talk is built from things I've lived, with the numbers where there are numbers.

What formats do you do?

Conference keynotes and breakouts (20 to 45 minutes), panels, podcasts and webinars, and private sessions for a leadership or revenue team. Private sessions are the format I like most: two hours, your numbers on the screen, and the team leaves with a build plan.

Do you charge?

Conferences and private team sessions, yes; the fee depends on format, prep, and travel, and I'll quote it on the first call. Podcasts, webinars, and community events are usually free.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

Atlanta. I travel for keynotes and team sessions anywhere in the US, and I do remote sessions anywhere.

How far ahead do you book?

Four weeks for a remote session, eight for anything with travel. If your date is closer than that, ask anyway.
